## 3. EQUITY OWNERSHIP, CAPITALIZATION & REVENUE DISTRIBUTION

### 3.1 Fixed Allocation Split (63% / 37%)
All economic benefits, net revenues, enterprise software licenses, institutional university/school contracts, telehealth facilitation earnings, government/NGO grants, sponsorship proceeds, and liquidation/merger/acquisition payouts shall be distributed strictly as follows:

* **Nithin Selvaraj (Party A):** **63.00% (Sixty-Three Percent)**
* **Arunachalam Premkumar (Party B):** **37.00% (Thirty-Seven Percent)**

### 3.2 Corporate Incorporation & Equity Capitalization
Upon legal entity incorporation (whether as a **Private Limited Company** under the *Companies Act, 2013*, a **Limited Liability Partnership** under the *LLP Act, 2008*, or a Delaware C-Corp for global expansion):
* The initial authorized and paid-up equity shares, founder stock, and voting rights shall strictly mirror the **63% (Nithin Selvaraj) : 37% (Arunachalam Premkumar)** capitalization table.
* Neither Party’s equity shall be diluted disproportionately without unanimous written consent.

### 3.3 Net Revenue Calculation & Disbursement Schedule
"Net Distributable Revenue" shall mean gross revenues received less bona fide direct third-party operating expenses (server hosting, cloud database instances, domain renewals, payment gateway fees, and mandatory taxes). All net proceeds shall be reconciled and disbursed **monthly or quarterly** directly into each Co-Founder's designated bank account in India.

## 4. IRREVOCABLE INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT (SECTION 19, COPYRIGHT ACT, 1957)

### 4.1 Comprehensive Assignment of Work Product
Pursuant to **Section 19 of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957**, each Co-Founder hereby **irrevocably, perpetually, and unconditionally assigns, transfers, and conveys** to the Haven Partnership all right, title, and interest in and to:
* All source code, Git repositories, frontend/backend architecture, and database schemas.
* All UI/UX designs, wireframes, logos, branding assets, and design systems.
* All registered and unregistered domain names (specifically including `havenwellbeing.in`).
* All algorithms, AI prompting pipelines, and confidential methodologies.
* This assignment is effective worldwide, across all media now known or hereafter created, in perpetuity.

### 4.2 Permanent Co-Founder Credits & Attribution
Platform documentation, corporate filings, and official media releases shall credit:
* **Arunachalam Premkumar:** *Initial Concept, Co-Founder & Strategy*
* **Nithin Selvaraj:** *Architecture, Engineering, Co-Founder & Lead Developer*

## 5. ROLES, GOVERNANCE & DEADLOCK RESOLUTION

### 5.1 Technical Authority — Nithin Selvaraj
Party A holds sole executive and operational decision-making authority over software architecture, code review, server infrastructure, cryptographic security, deployment pipelines, and technical hiring.

### 5.2 Strategic Direction — Arunachalam Premkumar
Party B holds primary leadership in concept curation, mental health framework research, youth organization outreach, and strategic partnerships.

### 5.3 Deadlock Resolution & Major Corporate Actions
* **General Operational & Technical Matters:** In the event of a disagreement on operational or product implementation, the decision of the **63% majority interest (Nithin Selvaraj)** shall be final and binding.
* **Fundamental Corporate Actions:** The following require mutual written consensus of both Co-Founders:
  1. Sale, merger, or liquidation of Haven.
  2. Incurring institutional debt exceeding ₹500,000 INR.
  3. Disproportionate issuance of new equity to third-party investors.

---

## 6. NON-CIRCUMVENTION, NON-COMPETE & CONFIDENTIALITY

### 6.1 Strict Non-Compete & Non-Circumvention (24 Months)
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of **twenty-four (24) months** following the withdrawal or departure of either Co-Founder:
* Neither Co-Founder shall directly or indirectly design, develop, fund, advise, or launch a competing adolescent mental health or peer counseling platform using Haven's proprietary concepts, architecture, or code.
* Neither Co-Founder shall solicit, divert, or attempt to take away any institutional clients, school partners, or licensed therapists from Haven.

### 6.2 Non-Disclosure of Confidential Information
Both Parties covenant to maintain the strict secrecy of all proprietary code, database credentials, encryption keys, business models, and user health data in perpetuity.

---

## 7. DEPARTURE, RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL (ROFR) & BUYOUT

### 7.1 Right of First Refusal (ROFR)
If either Co-Founder receives a bona fide third-party offer to purchase their equity, the other Co-Founder shall have the absolute first right to acquire that equity on identical financial terms.

### 7.2 Voluntary Departure / Buyout Formula
If a Co-Founder elects to withdraw from active participation, the remaining active Co-Founder shall have the exclusive option to purchase the departing Party’s equity at a fair market valuation determined by an independent certified chartered accountant or mutually agreed multiplier.

---

## 8. ENTIRE AGREEMENT & LEGAL SEVERABILITY

### 8.1 Integration / Entire Agreement Clause
This Agreement constitutes the **entire understanding and legal contract** between the Parties concerning Haven and **supersedes all prior verbal conversations, WhatsApp messages, Discord chats, notes, or informal understandings**.

### 8.2 Written Amendments Only
No amendment, waiver, or modification of any provision (specifically including the 63/37 split) shall be valid or enforceable unless executed in writing and signed by both Nithin Selvaraj and Arunachalam Premkumar.

### 8.3 Severability
If any court or arbitral tribunal finds any provision of this Agreement invalid or unenforceable, such determination shall not affect the validity of the remaining provisions, which shall remain in full force and effect.

* **Seat & Venue of Arbitration:** Republic of India.
